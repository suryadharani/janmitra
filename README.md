# JanMitra

> **Remembering the people who matter.**

JanMitra is a private personal web application being developed to help a Rishi preserve a meaningful digital record of important people he meets during his life and travels.

---

## 🌿 Purpose

The Rishi does not maintain a conventional contact list on his mobile phone. JanMitra is intended to help him record and remember the important individuals he encounters, capturing key details about each person and the context of their meeting. 

The eventual application will allow recording information such as:
- Name and contact details
- Photograph
- Date and location of the meeting
- Context, conversations, and significance of the encounter

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Policy

- **Private Personal Tool**: JanMitra is strictly a private personal record and reference system. It is not a public social network, public directory, or community platform.
- **No Real Personal Data in Public Repository**: This public repository does not contain any real person's personal information, private contact details, or photographs.

## ✨ Design Philosophy

JanMitra is built guided by the following principles:

- **Simple** — Clean, unobtrusive, and distraction-free interface.
- **Private** — Personal data security and quiet isolation by design.
- **Mobile-First** — Seamlessly accessible on phones, tablets, and desktop devices.
- **Easy to Use** — Intuitive entry and retrieval of meaningful records.
- **Dignified** — Respectful presentation honoring personal relationships.
- **Focused on Meaningful Connections** — Designed to preserve memory and context rather than superficial metrics.
